<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I started in journalism in Prishtina in 2013, first at the TV channel Klan Kosova, then for four and a half years at the news portal IndeksOnline. I reported daily on politics, society and the economy, and interviewed people from politics, public administration and civil society. At the same time, I studied journalism at the University of Prishtina.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After moving to Vienna, I began writing in 2019 for Albinfo, the Albanian-language outlet for Austria and Switzerland. Since then I have profiled doctors, researchers, entrepreneurs and students, and reported on integration, business and culture. In 2025 I added practical guides for HAJDE, a Vienna news service for people with roots in the Balkans.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Alongside work, I completed an MA in Communication Science at the University of Vienna. My thesis examined how financial challenges and the COVID-19 pandemic contributed to the closure of newspapers in Kosovo.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Since 2023 I have also built experience in leadership and operations: first looking after 25 apartments and their guests, and now as shift lead and deputy restaurant manager at Vapiano in Vienna. There I lead teams of up to 15 people and have trained more than 50 new colleagues.</p>
